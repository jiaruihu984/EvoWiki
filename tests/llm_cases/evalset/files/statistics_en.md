# Introductory statistics: practice handout

## Chapter 1: Observations

The three recorded patient waiting times are 2, 4, and 6 minutes.
These are synthetic teaching data, not patient records.

## Chapter 2: Arithmetic mean

The arithmetic mean is the sum of the observations divided by their count.
For the observations in Chapter 1, the mean is (2 + 4 + 6) / 3 = 4 minutes.

### Question 1

What is the arithmetic mean of 2, 4, and 6 minutes?

A. 3 minutes
B. 4 minutes
C. 12 minutes

Answer key: B.
